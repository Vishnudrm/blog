On Being Me

Personal note Long form

Plans for everything, started nothing, and somehow everything gets done anyway. The deadline passes, the thing exists, and nobody is more surprised than me. That is just how it works. I have stopped questioning it.

There is a shelf full of books chosen carefully and read almost never. They are waiting for a version of me with more patience than this one has managed so far. Music fills the silence that most people fill with other people. Cricket slows the afternoon down to exactly the right speed. A quiet room is not emptiness — it is the only place where things start to make sense.

Solitude is comfortable. Genuinely comfortable, not the kind that is secretly loneliness wearing a different coat. But the right people in the right moment change something in the room entirely. Energy comes from somewhere unexpected. The conversation goes longer than planned. Something gets said that needed saying. Those moments are rare. That is exactly what makes them matter.

There is a fear of saying the wrong thing, of knowing something halfway and being asked to defend the other half. So things get written down instead. Carefully. Slowly. In a place where the words can be fixed before anyone sees them. That is what this is.

Not the kind of person who gets noticed by someone who does not already know them. Two opposite things are almost always true at the same time. Comfortable with contradictions. Still figuring out everything else.
